Digital marketing teams often make the mistake of averaging performance metrics across radically different campaigns.

When a brand awareness video campaign running on CPM is combined into the same dashboard view as a conversion campaign optimized for purchase events on CPA, neither metric reflects true performance.

Cerebro DNano solves this structural flaw by grouping campaigns into explicit objective categories:
1. Awareness (Reach, Impressions, CPM)
2. Consideration (Link Clicks, CTR, CPC)
3. Conversions (Purchases, Cost Per Acquisition, ROAS)
