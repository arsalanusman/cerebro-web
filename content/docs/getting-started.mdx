Cerebro DNano connects to your advertising channels through official API endpoints using scoped read-only OAuth permissions.

## Authentication Steps

1. Navigate to the Connections tab inside your Cerebro workspace.
2. Select the channel you wish to authenticate: Meta Ads, Google Ads, or TikTok Ads.
3. Complete the single sign-on authorization modal presented by the respective platform.
4. Select the specific Ad Account IDs you want to import into your workspace.
5. Verify initial sync status in the Health & Status dashboard.
