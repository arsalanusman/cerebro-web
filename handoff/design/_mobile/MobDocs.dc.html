Before you begin

Cerebro accounts are set up with our team after a demo. You’ll receive an email invitation.

1. Accept your invitation

Open the invitation email and set your password. [CONFIRM: sign-in method]

2. Add a client

Each brand you report on is a client. Add its name and reporting currency.

3. Connect a platform

Open Connections, choose a platform, sign in, and pick the ad accounts to include.
