Blended ROAS vs platform ROAS: which number should you trust?

Every platform claims credit for the same sale. Here’s how to report both numbers honestly.

Why platform totals never add up

Meta, Google and TikTok each measure conversions with their own attribution windows and models. When one customer sees ads on two platforms, both can count the same sale.

What blended ROAS measures

Blended ROAS divides the revenue you actually recorded by your total ad spend across every platform.

The formula
Blended ROAS = total recorded revenue ÷ total ad spend, across all platforms.

How to report both, honestly

  1. Lead with blended ROAS for budget decisions.
  2. Show platform ROAS beside it, labeled as the platform’s claim.
  3. Explain the gap in one sentence, every time.
