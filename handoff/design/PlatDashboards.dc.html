Questions about dashboards

Can clients see their own dashboards?

Yes. Client viewers see their own dashboards and nothing else.

Can we export the data behind a dashboard?

Yes, as CSV. Large exports, up to a million rows, run in the background.

Can we track budgets?

Yes. Budget pacing shows spend against plan for each period.
