We are preparing this SaaS website for submission to the Google for Startups Cloud Program. The objective is to transform the current landing page from a visually appealing prototype into a startup website that demonstrates product maturity, credibility, and a clear business model.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT redesign the overall visual style.
- Preserve the current branding, layout, typography, spacing, color palette, components, and design system.
- Keep the existing aesthetic and only improve content structure, UX, and trust signals.
- Avoid using placeholder or generic marketing copy.

The website should clearly communicate within the first 5 seconds:

• What the product is
• Who it is for
• What problem it solves
• Why it is different
• Why customers should trust it
