# IMPORTANT

This document defines the identity, personality, design philosophy, and visual direction of Cerebro.

Use this document as the primary reference before designing any webpage.

The uploaded PNG logo is the official logo and must always be treated as the single source of truth.

Do not redesign or reinterpret the logo.

---

# Product Overview

Cerebro is an enterprise SaaS platform that unifies advertising data from multiple digital platforms into one intelligent reporting system.

Supported platforms include:

- Meta
- Google Ads
- TikTok
- DV360

Future platforms will continue to be added.

The platform automatically gathers campaign data, normalizes KPIs, and provides agencies with one reliable source of truth.

The website should communicate:

- intelligence
- trust
- automation
- precision
- operational clarity

Never make the product feel like another analytics dashboard.

Instead, position it as the operating system behind modern digital agencies.
